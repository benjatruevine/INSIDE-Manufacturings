About Us

About KIBOOYA SCRAP YARD

KIBOOYA SCRAP YARD is a scrap materials dealer based along Sonde–Sseta–Namungongo Road in Uganda. We deal in scrap metals, plastics, polythene, waste paper, and used iron sheets.

We provide a contact point for people and businesses looking to discuss the sale or purchase of these materials. Whether you have metal offcuts, old iron sheets, recyclable plastics, or waste paper, get in touch to discuss the materials available and the next steps.

Our goal is to make scrap materials trading straightforward through direct communication and clear discussions about material types, quantities, and pricing.
